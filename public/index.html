Playing solo? Either keyboard layout works, and Space also jumps.

🎯 Shooting: hold shoot to rise up, then let go when the meter is in the green zone. A perfect release from a good shooter almost always drops. Contested shots and deep threes are harder.
💥 Dunks & layups: press shoot while driving close to the rim. High-flyers dunk from farther out. Defenders who jump at the right moment can reject it.
👊 Hit: press Hit next to your opponent to knock them flat and send the ball flying. Miss and you're left off-balance, and a well-timed crossover makes you whiff. Knocked-down players get a moment of immunity after they get up.
🧤 Blocks: jump just after the shooter rises. Big men have extra block range.
🌀 Crossovers: a quick burst past your defender. Good ball handlers can break ankles and drop the defender for a second.
🏀 2v2: pass to your teammate. Pass to a high-flyer cutting to the rim for an ALLEY-OOP. Press pass without the ball to call for it.
💨 Nitro: hold to sprint. It drains the blue stamina bar, which refills when you stop.
⚡ SUPER bar: fills with buckets, knockdowns, blocks and ankle-breakers. When it's full, press Super:
• With the ball → MEGA DUNK. Fly in from anywhere for a guaranteed, unblockable slam (3 points from behind the arc) that knocks defenders down.
• On defense → IRON DEFENSE. An 8-second shield: you can't be knocked down or crossed over, your hits always land, and nearby shots get auto-blocked.
Archetypes: every star has a small passive edge:
🃏 Action cards (optional) float onto the court. Run or jump through one to grab it: Mega Dunk 💥 (fills your Super bar), Iron Defense 🛡️, Fast Break ⚡ (speed and unlimited nitro), Super Shot 🔥 (your next shot can't miss or be blocked), Freeze ❄️.
